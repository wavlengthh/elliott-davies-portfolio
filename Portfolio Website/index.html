I’m building my craft as a music producer and mixing/mastering engineer while pursuing a B.S. in Audio Production at Full Sail University, graduating in January 2028. My work is focused on making, mixing, and mastering music with intention, character, and impact.

With an intuitive ear for both live production and in-the-box mixing and mastering, I bridge the gap between stage and studio. I know how music needs to translate— whether driving energy through a live PA or delivering clarity, impact, and balance on a finished record.

I also work live production and stagecraft at Walton Arts Center / Walmart AMP, learning signal flow, live audio systems, and how professional shows run from the inside — knowledge I apply directly to every mix and production.
